# Brand Guidelines

## Brand promise

Deliver clear, actionable climate resilience decisions — not just data.

## Voice and tone

- Clear and practical
- Evidence-led and transparent
- Calm and credible under uncertainty

## Visual direction

- Deep, high-contrast tones for trust and clarity
- Accent colors to indicate risk severity and intervention priority
- Data-first typography with strong readability for dashboards

## Messaging pillars

1. Understand climate exposure quickly
2. Prioritize interventions with confidence
3. Execute and track resilience outcomes
