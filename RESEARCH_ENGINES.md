# Research Engines and Suggestions

## Research engine catalog

### 1) Hazard intelligence engine
- Purpose: continuously identify local climate hazards by geography and asset class.
- Inputs: IPCC, NOAA, Copernicus, local hazard maps, flood/heat/wildfire records.
- Output artifacts: risk layer snapshots, exposure rankings, and intervention trigger thresholds.

### 2) Intervention matching engine
- Purpose: map each risk profile to practical mitigation/adaptation interventions.
- Inputs: building type, infrastructure class, local climate intensity, budget constraints.
- Output artifacts: ranked intervention shortlist with confidence and implementation notes.

### 3) Policy and incentive engine
- Purpose: discover grants, tax incentives, and regulatory requirements that change project economics.
- Inputs: jurisdiction, sector, project type, timeline.
- Output artifacts: incentive matrix and compliance checklist.

### 4) Commercial viability engine
- Purpose: connect resilience measures to revenue, margin protection, and total-cost outcomes.
- Inputs: capex/opex assumptions, outage risk, insurance impacts, customer targets.
- Output artifacts: payback estimates, ARR scenarios, and adoption funnel assumptions.

## Suggested intervention modules

- Heat pumps and electrification retrofits
- Solar + battery storage and distributed generation
- Flood barriers, drainage upgrades, and water management
- Blue/green infrastructure: roof gardens, reflective surfaces, tree canopy
- Atmospheric water generation in water-stressed areas
- Green AI infrastructure and lower-carbon hosting strategies

## Suggestion prioritization framework

Score each suggestion using:
1. Risk reduction impact (1-5)
2. Deployment speed (1-5)
3. Capital efficiency (1-5)
4. Policy/incentive fit (1-5)
5. Operational maintainability (1-5)

`Priority Score = (Impact + Speed + Capital + Incentive + Maintainability) / 5`
