# Go-To-Market (S2M)

## Positioning

Climate Resilience Navigator helps organizations move from climate data overload to prioritized, budget-ready resilience actions.

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

- Municipal resilience teams
- Utility and infrastructure operators
- Real-estate and campus operators with climate exposure

## Priority interventions catalog (research engine scope)

- Heat pumps and building electrification
- Solar + storage and distributed generation
- Flood mitigation and water resilience
- Nature-based systems (cool roofs, roof gardens, urban canopy)
- Atmospheric water generation where water stress is acute
- Green AI operations and lower-carbon compute hosting for climate analytics

## Revenue model

1. SaaS subscriptions (tiered users + data modules)
2. Enterprise integrations (API + data onboarding)
3. Advisory/implementation services

## 3-year objective framing

- Target outcome: $10M ARR in 3 years
- Path: land municipal/infra lighthouse customers, expand multi-site deployments, and upsell analytics modules

## Launch plan

1. Launch Vercel-hosted website-in-test for buyer validation
2. Pilot with a narrow regional climate-risk dataset
3. Ship role-based workflows (operator, planner, executive)
4. Add Stripe-managed conversion and renewal funnel
